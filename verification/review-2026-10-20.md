# Independent implementation review

The original GPT-6.1 Sol implementation was reviewed by the root coordinator
and a separate GPT-6 Astra agent. This is technical review, not market or
PowerPoint interoperability evidence.

## Findings and fixes

1. XML parts with an uppercase extension were silently omitted. Selection
   now follows declared XML content types, with case-insensitive `.xml`
   recognition as a fallback. A content-typed `.part` slide is also included.
2. Relationship types were recognized by their final URI segment. Custom
   `/theme` relationships could affect mappings. Only full recognized
   transitional/strict Office relationship types and attribute namespaces
   now participate.
3. Old animation-frame callbacks and demo downloads could replace a newer
   selection. Every asynchronous scan boundary is generation-checked.
   The standalone build additionally embeds its original demo, eliminating
   the demo network request.

Unit regressions cover part identity and official namespace recognition.
The portable browser regression covers source selection and stale callbacks.

## Independent evidence

- A separately exported original diagnostic presentation was modified to
  contain two hidden master font references. A Python/lxml XPath oracle
  counted 363 stored references; the app's full part/font multiset matched
  in Chrome 155 and Firefox 153, including both exact level-3 locations.
- Renaming a slide to uppercase `.XML` and then to a content-typed `.part`
  preserved the expected reference count after the fix.
- A decoy relationship with a custom URI no longer changed the Office theme
  mapping.
- The original stale animation-frame and delayed-demo reproductions passed
  after the guards in both browsers.
- Corrupt input hides the old result; filters, JSON exports, keyboard
  disclosure and 390px layout were checked.

`independent-reference.json` and `fixtures/independent-master-font.pptx`
preserve the separate oracle case. It is synthetic and contains no customer
material. The base was generated with the bundled artifact-tool exporter;
it was not opened in Microsoft PowerPoint. Native rendering/repair success
and actual missing-font warnings remain unverified.

Raw workstation logs and temporary screenshots remain outside public source
archives. Final build/offline regression records are separate from the
earlier development-server checks.
